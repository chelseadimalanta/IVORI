# Solenne

Modest activewear for women in Canada. Modest in cut, bold in motion.

ITM550 Advanced E-Business project by Chelsea Dimalanta (formerly MIS.Co).

**Live site:** https://chelseadimalanta.github.io/solenne/

## What works

- Home, shop, product, coverage & size guide, bag and checkout pages
- Search, and filters by category, activity, size and colour
- A coverage card on every product page
- Bag with quantities, a free-shipping progress bar and discount codes (`SOLENNE10`, `CREATOR15`)
- Set bundle: 10% off when the bag has a top plus a bottom or skirt
- Limited drop collection (the Clay Capsule)
- Checkout with tax by province, in CAD. It's a prototype, so no payment is taken.

## Files

- `index.html`: the whole site, with no build step
- `AI-PROMPTS.md`: Gen-AI prompts and references, as the course requires
- `images/`: product photos, once they're generated (see AI-PROMPTS.md)
