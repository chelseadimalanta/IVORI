# IVORI

Natural oral care in metal you keep, with refills that come to you.

ITM550 Advanced E-Business project by Chelsea Dimalanta. The project pivoted from Solenne (modest activewear) to IVORI in November 2026.

## What works

- Home, shop, product, How refills work, bag and checkout pages
- Search, plus filters by category and by subscription, refillable, travel and limited drop
- A "What's inside" card on every product: key ingredients, what it's free from, packaging and how long it lasts
- Product options: flavour, size, finish, and starter or refill
- Subscribe and save 15%, delivered every 1, 2 or 3 months
- Discount codes `IVORI10` and `CREATOR15`, applied to one-time items only so they never stack with subscriptions
- The Switch Kit bundle and the Black Cherry limited drop
- Checkout with tax by province, in CAD. It's a prototype, so no payment is taken.

## Files

- `index.html`: the whole site, with no build step
- `images/`: compressed photos used by the site
- `assets/`: original Google Gemini images
